CaSP-SARC

A dedicated sarcoma stream conducting genomic screening and building evidence for future clinical trials.

Earlier relapse detection

Research seeking more reliable ways to identify children at risk of osteosarcoma relapse.

Clinical nurse fellowship

Building specialist bone and soft-tissue sarcoma nursing capability at RPA Sydney.

Fergus McCulla Emotional and Psychological Support Programme

While undergoing treatment for sarcoma, Fergus McCulla spoke openly about the need for emotional and psychological support from diagnosis onwards.

Named in his honour, the programme offers free emotional and psychological support to those living with sarcoma and their families, with oversight from experienced clinicians.

Cooper's legacy

“Sarcoma does not have the research to support a definitive cure.”
Cooper Rice-Brading · The Great Hall, University of Sydney, November 2016

At 17, Cooper saw the disparity between sarcoma and other childhood cancers and made it his mission to change the story for the people who came after him.
