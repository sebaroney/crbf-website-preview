Emotional and psychological support

Fergus McCulla Emotional and Psychological Support Programme

In honour of Fergus

While being treated for rhabdomyosarcoma, Fergus McCulla spoke openly about the need for emotional and psychological support from the point of diagnosis. By sharing his own experience, he helped others facing sarcoma feel less alone. Fergus died in August 2021, and CRBF named the programme in his honour.

Who the programme supports

The programme offers free emotional and psychological support to those living with sarcoma and their families. It complements CRBF’s peer support and is supervised by experienced clinicians.

How it began

Clinical psychologist Louise Hird and psychiatrist Dr Lahvinya Kulaendra helped develop the programme in response to gaps in support identified by families and the CRBF team. The programme launched in December 2021.
