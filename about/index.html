About CRBF

A young man's vision became a community's purpose.

CRBF was established to honour Cooper Rice-Brading's determination to change the trajectory for people diagnosed with sarcoma.

Cooper's story

One determined foot in front of the other.

Cooper was a sport-loving teenager approaching his final year at Sydney Grammar School when, at 17, he was diagnosed with osteosarcoma. During treatment, he discovered the stark disparity between sarcoma and other childhood cancers.

Cooper wanted to change that. His vision now guides a foundation committed to funding research, raising awareness and improving the patient journey for families across Australia.

“Sarcoma does not have the research to support a definitive cure.”

Our purpose

Hope, research and practical support.

Fund research

Direct community generosity toward clinical research with the potential to improve diagnosis, treatment and survival.

Support families

Help people living with sarcoma and those closest to them navigate the emotional and practical realities of diagnosis.

Build awareness

Make sarcoma better understood so symptoms are recognised and people reach specialist care sooner.
