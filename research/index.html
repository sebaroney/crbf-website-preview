Research

Clinical research is the key to critical change.

Sarcoma is a rare and complex cancer that disproportionately affects children, adolescents and young adults. For too many, treatment options remain limited, therapies can be devastating, and survival outcomes have not improved enough.

Because sarcoma is rare, research funding is often scarce. Yet the need for progress is urgent.

Research is the key to earlier diagnosis, more effective treatments, fewer lifelong side effects and, ultimately, more lives saved.

Every breakthrough begins with an investment. By funding innovative research and strengthening collaboration between Australia's leading scientists and clinicians, we can accelerate discoveries and bring new treatment opportunities to those who desperately need them.

Those diagnosed with sarcoma deserve more than hope. They deserve better treatment options, better outcomes and the chance of a future.

Your support can help make that possible.
