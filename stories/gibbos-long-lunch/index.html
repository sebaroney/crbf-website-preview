Gibbo's Long Lunch

The lush green pastures surrounding the beautiful Manning River provided the perfect setting for @gibbos_long_lunch yesterday. The sun shone brightly, spirits were high, and the magnificent marquee nestled in the countryside provided a wonderful setting for a truly extraordinary afternoon.

Once again, @suziec124 , Neil, @harrygibsonn_ and @lilyygibson brought together an incredible community of family, friends and supporters for a magnificent celebration of love, friendship and generosity, held in honour of their much-loved son and brother, Jack.

Jack’s legacy lives on through the CRBF Jack Gibson Sarcoma Patient & Family Support Programme, which proudly bears his name and continues to provide vital assistance to young people living with sarcoma and the families who stand beside them.

The food was exceptional, the company wonderful and the generosity of guests simply overwhelming. But what made the afternoon so special was the unmistakable love for Jack that brought everyone together.

Our heartfelt thanks to the Gibson family for once again hosting such a beautiful and flawlessly organised occasion, and to every person who contributed so generously to its success.

Jack’s absence is deeply felt, but the love he inspired continues to make an extraordinary difference in the lives of others.
