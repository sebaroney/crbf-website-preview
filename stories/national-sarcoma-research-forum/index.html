National CAYA Sarcoma Research Forum

For the first time, leading Australian sarcoma researchers, clinicians, trial experts, government, philanthropists, advocates, allied health professionals & those with lived experience came together in one room.

Not to compete. Not to work in isolation. To collaborate.

CRBF was proud to bring this exceptional group together for the inaugural National Childhood, Adolescent & Young Adult (CAYA) Sarcoma Research Forum in Melbourne.

Among them were families who had lost loved ones to sarcoma. They reminded us why progress cannot wait.

Together, we challenged assumptions, identified gaps in research & clinical trials, & explored ways to connect expertise and resources nationally. Our aim? Moving the dial over the coming decade.

Funded through the generosity of the Weinert family & convened by Dr Graham Hall of Synergy Health Solutions, the Forum united the sector in pursuit of better outcomes.

Heartfelt thanks to Dr Hall, @julrmolloy , Trish Henry, Jeremy Sutcliffe @soot57 , @keith.cox5 , Mandy Basson @sockittosarcoma , Colin Brading & Mitch Rice-Brading for their tireless work behind the scenes..

To every participant who made time in demanding clinical & personal schedules to attend, listen and contribute: thank you. You made the day matter.

Sincere thanks to Adam Lambert and Jonathan Godin at Cancer Australia and @healthgovau for joining us. Your presence meant so much, especially to families with lived experience. Bringing government, clinicians, researchers & families together is vital. We deeply value your engagement.

Thank you to Lachlan Ellis at PARKROYALMELBOURNE & Daniel Littman at @ENCOREEVENTTECHNOLOGY for making the day run smoothly.

Special thanks to Mr Peter Weinert & the Weinert family for making this Forum possible.

Sarcoma is rare. No single organisation can overcome these challenges alone.

Now comes the work of turning conversation into action.

For every young person facing sarcoma, every family alongside them, and everyone carrying the heartbreak of loss, this collaboration is deeply personal. It is about honouring lives, creating hope & giving future generations the chance of a different story.
