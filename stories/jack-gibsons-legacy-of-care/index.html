Jack Gibson’s Legacy of Care

Some people leave an impression that lasts a lifetime. Jack Gibson was one of them.

1 December 2021, saw Jack stand before 250 guests at the CRBF Collaboration Corporate Breakfast, delivering one of the most poignant & courageous speeches we have ever had the privilege of hearing.

He was facing unimaginable personal adversity, yet he spoke with extraordinary honesty about his determination to live, his refusal to let cancer define him, & his desire to make the very most of every day. He encouraged the room to do the same. There was not a soul untouched by his courage, his dignity or his remarkable spirit. He was magnificent.

Today, Jack’s family & friends mark 4 years without him. 4 years of an absence that words can’t describe. 4 years of longing for one more moment with a much-loved son, brother,& friend.

Through their heartbreak, Jack’s family has found the incredible strength to ensure his compassion for others’ lives. 7 Nov sees the family welcome 200 people to their home, hosting a lunch in his honour, & raising funds for the Jack Gibson Sarcoma Patient & Family Support Programme.

It takes extraordinary courage to do what Jack did that December morning. It takes extraordinary love & strength to do what his family continues to do in his memory.

Jack understood suffering in a way no young person ever should. Yet even in the midst of his own adversity, he was thinking about others & how he might make their journey a little easier, a measure of this very special young man. It is the legacy we are so privileged to help carry forward.

Please honour Jack in a very practical way. Buy a raffle ticket. Bid on an auction item. Donate.

Every contribution will help the programme support people facing sarcoma & the families walking this extraordinarily difficult road beside them.

To Suzie, Neil, Harry, Lily & those who love & miss Jack so deeply, we hope you can take comfort in knowing just how much he touched the lives of others. His courage inspired us, his compassion humbled us, & his extraordinary spirit remains with us.

Four years. A lifetime of love & a legacy that will continue to change lives.

Jack Gibson’s Legacy of Care

“In February 2017, Jack Gibson, at the age of 20, was diagnosed with Osteosarcoma. Jack was about to enter his third year at university and was living his best life. He was one of almost 2000 people diagnosed with sarcoma that year.

Sarcoma, while rare, is the most common cause of cancer related death in people under the age of 24.

Despite his best efforts, and those of his medical staff, family and friends, Jack passed away on November 1, 2022, almost 6 years later.

CRBF established a Sarcoma Patient and Family Support Programme and Jack, as Patient Advocate, provided his insight into the torrid and brutal journey that patients and families endure in search of the elusive recovery from Sarcoma.

Jack was passionate about helping those patients and their families who have or will in the future, be diagnosed with sarcoma.

Since his death, Jack’s family and fabulous group of friends have continued to work to establish a fund to support CRBF’s patient support programme that was renamed in his honour as the Jack Gibson Sarcoma Patient and Family Support Programme (JGSP&FSP). 

This fund aims to support the programme in perpetuity and thus continue to help those most in need of assistance while navigating a sarcoma diagnosis.

Due to the generosity of so many, to date over $500,000 has been raised but Jack’s family and friends are aiming to raise $1M to ensure the Fund’s longevity.” – Suzie Carlon & Neil Gibson (Jack’s parents)  

CRBF remains deeply and eternally grateful to the Gibson family and their loyal supporters for their unwavering commitment in reaching this extraordinary milestone. This vital funding will help secure the longevity of the Jack Gibson Sarcoma Patient & Family Support Programme, ensuring that patients and their families continue to receive the personalised, compassionate care they so richly deserve, both now and into the future.

If you or someone you know could benefit from specialised support through the JGSP&FSP please go to: https://www.crbf.org.au/patient-support/
