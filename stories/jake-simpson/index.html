Jake Simpson

Jake loved football, his club and time with his mates. From the age of 15, he called his mum Karen “Mar”. Years later, the words he used to sign off, “Love ya Mar”, became the name of the Mother’s Day breakfast she organised in his memory.

Jake died from Ewing sarcoma at 22. Through the breakfasts, his family and the Toowoomba community raised $25,000 for the Jake Simpson Memorial Ewing Sarcoma Research Fund, helping support research into the cancer that took his life.
