Beatrix Victoria Banks Sarcoma Research Fund

Beatrix (Bea) Victoria Banks’ story is not defined by the cancer that took her life, but by the extraordinary joy, love & colour she brought to the world during her precious nearly eleven years.

A cherished daughter, sister, granddaughter, cousin & friend, Bea was bright, funny, creative, fiercely loyal & wonderfully determined.

She believed in magic, fairies, unicorns & rainbows. She organised tea parties, built cubbies & filled her home with music, dancing & her contagious giggle. She noticed the little things so many of us overlook; a feather on the ground, a flower in the garden, the flutter of a butterfly. Through her eyes, the ordinary became extraordinary.

Too often, when we remember children who have died from cancer, their illness becomes the story we tell. Yet they were children first, with dreams, friendships, favourite things & beautiful lives that deserve to be remembered.

While Bea’s family & all who loved her live with the immeasurable heartbreak of her absence, her magic remains. In every butterfly, every rainbow & every small act of kindness, there is a reminder of a little girl whose life was far too short, but whose impact will last forever.

To honour Bea’s memory, her family, Emily, Duncan & Harvey, have established the Beatrix Victoria Banks Sarcoma Research Fund, initially supporting vital paediatric sarcoma research at the Hudson Institute of Medical Research, led by Professor Ron Firestein.

Through Bea’s Fund, her legacy will help drive research towards better treatments, bring hope to children & families, and ultimately help find a cure.

Please support Bea’s Fund in whatever way you can. Every contribution helps turn Bea’s legacy into hope for other children.
