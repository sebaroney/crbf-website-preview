How the Foundation is governed

Volunteers donate their time and expertise, and the Foundation pays no salaries. Tax-deductible donations fund sarcoma research, support those living with sarcoma and their families, and raise awareness of sarcoma nationwide.

Board oversight

CRBF is governed by a Board of eight directors, including a Chairperson. The Board meets quarterly, and a Medical Advisory Board of four members provides specialist advice pertaining to funding proposals.

Meet the board

Constitution

Our Constitution sets out the Foundation’s charitable purpose: to fund sarcoma research, raise awareness and support those undergoing treatment for sarcoma. It also sets out the Board’s duties and how the Foundation manages its funds.
