Cooper Rice-Brading Foundation

Governance

The Cooper Rice-Brading Foundation is registered with the Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC). ABN 50 623 689 109.

How the Foundation is governed

Volunteers donate their time and expertise, and the Foundation pays no salaries. Tax-deductible donations fund sarcoma research, support those living with sarcoma and their families, and raise awareness of sarcoma nationwide.

Board and committees

CRBF has a board of eight directors. Two committees report to the board. One oversees media, marketing and public relations. The other oversees fundraising.
