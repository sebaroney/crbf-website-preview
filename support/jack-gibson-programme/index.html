The Jack Gibson Sarcoma Patient & Family Support Programme

A sarcoma diagnosis can change what a family needs from one week to the next. CRBF’s national programme offers confidential support shaped around your circumstances.

Jack helped shape this programme by sharing what mattered to him while he was living with osteosarcoma.

Read Jack’s story

Practical help at home and during treatment

Help can include meals at home, grocery and petrol vouchers, support for carers and help accessing equipment such as a mobility aid or a hospital bed.

Financial support

The team can discuss treatment-related costs, including medical gap payments and out-of-pocket fertility treatment expenses. Funeral assistance may also be available to families experiencing financial hardship.

Someone to talk to

Families can connect with someone who has lived experience of sarcoma, request pastoral care or access free psychological support through the Fergus McCulla programme. Support can extend into grief and bereavement.

Help finding your way through care

CRBF can help you prepare questions for your treating team, navigate government services and identify practical next steps. You can start by telling the team what would make the greatest difference for you right now.
