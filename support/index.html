Patient and family support

You do not have to navigate sarcoma alone.

The Jack Gibson Sarcoma Patient & Family Support Programme helps patients and families find practical, emotional and psychological support throughout the sarcoma journey.

How we can help

Support that begins by listening.

Tell us what is happening

Contact the CRBF support team and share as much or as little as feels right.

Connect with support

The team can help identify services, information and practical next steps suited to your circumstances.

Stay connected

Support can continue through diagnosis, treatment, recovery and the challenges families face beyond.

Email the support teamContact form

If this is urgent

CRBF is not an emergency or crisis service.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call emergency services. For urgent medical advice, contact your treating team or an appropriate health service.
